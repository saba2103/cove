# Our home — `lib/features/home/home_settings_screen.dart`

Reference: `our-home.png` · Budget: 4

The Front Door. The most decorative screen in the app and deliberately so.

## Order
1. Arch illustration with the city inside it, names beneath, the "with Lyla, Cabo & Luca" line.
2. Four Almanac-style `CoveMetricCard`s.
3. "Who lives here" — people at `CoveSize.avatarProfile`, each pet in its own bubble.

## Rules
- **Names are not editable here.** They come from profiles; one person creates the home and the other joins.
- The gradient scrolls with the **illustration**, not with the page.
- No role captions under the people. The arch says it.
