- **Batch 4.3c — Approved-Code Invites; the Server Never Sees the Key (`Oct 26, 2026`)**:
  - *Outcome*:
    1. **Keyless 6-Digit Pairing Codes via `libsodium` & Removal of `homes.description` Fallback (`design/specs/17-invite-and-keys.md` §1, §2, §4)**: Updated [`HomeController.createPairingCode`](../lib/features/home/home_controller.dart) and [`PairingQrView`](../lib/sync/key_management/pairing_qr_view.dart) so tapping **Send a key** creates a keyless `pairing_codes` row (`code` via `sodium.randombytes`, `home_id`, `inviter_id`, `expires_at` = now + 10 min, `status = 'open'`) with no payload and no key, while the in-person QR continues carrying the payload directly from the inviter's screen to the joiner's camera. Removed all `homes.description` `pairing:` fallback reads and writes.
    2. **`libsodium` Sealed Boxes (`crypto.box.keyPair()`, `crypto.box.seal`, `crypto.box.sealOpen`), 22-Bit Two-Word SAS (`AMBER · HARBOUR`), and In-Memory Key Pinning**: Added [`lib/sync/key_management/pairing_wordlist.dart`](../lib/sync/key_management/pairing_wordlist.dart) (2,048-word list), [`lib/sync/key_management/pairing_crypto.dart`](../lib/sync/key_management/pairing_crypto.dart) (backed directly by `sodium_libs` initialized once at startup in [`lib/main.dart`](../lib/main.dart), plus `SHA-256(joiner_public_key ‖ code)` 22-bit two-word derivation), [`lib/sync/key_management/pairing_relay.dart`](../lib/sync/key_management/pairing_relay.dart), and [`lib/sync/key_management/pairing_approval_sheet.dart`](../lib/sync/key_management/pairing_approval_sheet.dart). The inviter derives the two words from the public key held in memory, re-reads the `pairing_requests` row just before sealing, automatically declines with `"This request changed. Ask them to try again."` if the row changed, and seals to that exact in-memory key.
    3. **Supabase Migration Proven Against Live Schema Dump (`supabase/migrations/20261026000000_approved_code_invites.sql`, `supabase/tests/approved_code_invites_rls_test.sql`)**: Added the SQL migration creating keyless `public.pairing_codes` (readable/deletable only by the inviter), `public.create_pairing_code(target_home_id)` (deletes the inviter's own expired codes first and retries up to 5 times on a primary-key collision), `public.lookup_pairing_code(code)` (`SECURITY DEFINER` reading `home_members.display_name` with `auth.users` metadata fallback, returning only `home_name`, `inviter_name`, `status` for open unexpired codes, and rate-limiting failed lookups to 5/hour/user), and `public.pairing_requests` with RLS (max 3 requests/code, max 5 requests/hour/user, delete restricted to joiner or inviter) and `trg_pairing_requests_enforce_update` (`code`, `joiner_id`, `joiner_public_key` immutable after insert; joiner may only set `status = 'done'`; only inviter may set `sealed_payload`, `'approved'`, or `'declined'`). Also updated `HomeController.createPairingCode` to delete the inviter's own expired codes first and retry up to 5 times on a primary-key collision.
  - *Under the Hood*:
    - Added end-to-end, `libsodium` direct seal/sealOpen interop, re-read key-change detection, expired-code cleanup + 5x PK collision retry, and server rule tests in [`test/approved_code_invite_43c_test.dart`](../test/approved_code_invite_43c_test.dart), and proven [`supabase/migrations/20261026000000_approved_code_invites.sql`](../supabase/migrations/20261026000000_approved_code_invites.sql) + [`supabase/tests/approved_code_invites_rls_test.sql`](../supabase/tests/approved_code_invites_rls_test.sql) against the dumped `public` schema from `dklecfmiccevnkhxnwmo` on a throwaway local PostgreSQL instance.
